✓ MISSION SUCCESS VALKYRIE-2A Latest
Valkyrie-2A complete — first crewed Valkyrie flight a success, crew returns safely

Valkyrie-2A has completed its 3-day Kerbin orbital shakedown. The crew of two performed a full Command Module systems check, completed all assigned objectives, and returned safely to Kerbin. It is the first crewed flight of the Valkyrie programme. The Safety Review Board has cleared the mission and issued sign-off for Valkyrie-2B — the first crewed Mun orbital — to proceed to scheduling.

👨‍🚀
✓ MISSION SUCCESS VALKYRIE-1B ▶ PHASE II INITIATED Latest
Valkyrie-1B success — heat shield validated, Phase II initiated, crew certification unlocked

Valkyrie-1B has completed its uncrewed Command Module reentry test. The heat shield performed nominally at full lunar-return velocity and splashdown was confirmed on schedule. The Safety Review Board has formally closed Phase I of the Valkyrie programme and issued sign-off to proceed. Phase II — Crewed Kerbin & Mun Orbital — is now officially initiated. Valkyrie-2A, the first crewed Valkyrie flight, is next. Kerbonaut assignment and crew certification are now underway.

🏆
✓ MISSION SUCCESS VALKYRIE-1A Latest
Valkyrie-1A completes Block I launch vehicle test — Mun programme stack validated

Valkyrie-1A has completed its uncrewed test flight successfully. The Block I launch vehicle flew a full ascent profile, achieved clean stage separation, and completed reentry as planned. The stack is confirmed flight-worthy. With Phase I's first gate cleared, Valkyrie-1B — the uncrewed Command Module reentry test — is now the next milestone before any crew can be assigned to the programme.

🚀
◼ OFFICIALLY RETIRED ECOSTRAT-1 Latest
EcoStrat-1 officially retired — power failures and limited capability cited

Horizon Aerospace has formally retired EcoStrat-1, ending the satellite's operational life and permanently closing all communication channels. A post-mission review concluded that the spacecraft suffered recurring power failures every time it entered the dark side of Kerbin, caused by insufficient power storage capacity and undersized solar arrays that could not sustain operations without direct sunlight. Combined with its very limited instrumentation suite, EcoStrat-1 was never designed to be a long-term science platform. It was launched as an early agency achievement and a temporary atmospheric survey tool — and it fulfilled that role. All mission data has been archived. Horizon engineers will apply the lessons learned to future satellite designs. The spacecraft will remain in orbit until natural decay.

📡
✓ MISSION SUCCESS ECOSTRAT-3 Latest
EcoStrat-3 success — first satellite to reach orbit on nuclear engines

EcoStrat-3 has successfully reached Kerbin equatorial orbit and is fully operational, marking two historic firsts: it is the direct replacement for the retired EcoStrat-1, and it is the first satellite in Horizon Aerospace history — and possibly the first in Kerbin's history — to use nuclear engines to achieve orbit. The dual redundant power systems have been verified, ensuring the satellite will maintain full operation through the dark side of Kerbin without the power failures that plagued its predecessor. All atmospheric and climate sensors are confirmed nominal.

⚛️
⚠ PARTIAL SUCCESS ABLE-2 Recent
ABLE-2 partial success — reusability confirmed possible, but beyond current Horizon capability

ABLE-2 has returned a mixed result. The mission successfully demonstrated that reusable rocket technology is physically achievable — a significant scientific finding. However, the data also made clear that Horizon Aerospace's current engineering understanding is insufficient to implement it reliably and safely. The Safety Review Board has classified the mission as a partial success and has recommended a full programme review before any further reusable vehicle flights.

⚠️
✓ MISSION SUCCESS ABLE-1 Latest
ABLE-1 crewed test flight successful — Jebediah & Greging Kerman land outside KSC

Horizon Aerospace's ABLE program has completed its first crewed test flight. Astronauts Jebediah Kerman and Greging Kerman launched aboard ABLE-1, flew the vehicle through its complete design test profile, and successfully landed outside of Kerbin Space Center. Both crew members are confirmed safe and in good health. The full propulsion stack, airframe, and guidance systems performed as designed. Engineering teams are now reviewing flight data ahead of ABLE-2 iteration planning.

✓ MISSION SUCCESS ECOSTRAT-2 Latest
EcoStrat-2 achieves polar orbit — surface mapping campaign underway

EcoStrat-2 has successfully reached its target polar orbit around Kerbin. All high-resolution optical and spectral imaging instruments have been powered on and confirmed operational by ground stations. The 30-day full-surface mapping campaign is now underway. This is Horizon Aerospace's third consecutive successful mission.

✓ MISSION SUCCESS ECOSTRAT-0 Latest
EcoStrat-0 — First crewed Horizon flight succeeds. Kerbonauts reach space.

In a historic first for Horizon Aerospace, EcoStrat-0 has completed successfully. The mission marked the agency's first crewed launch, with Kerbonauts reaching space and returning safely to Kerbin. The Safety Review Board has confirmed all mission objectives were met and the crew is in good health. This is the first crewed spaceflight carried out under the Horizon Aerospace name — and the clearest signal yet that the new agency has the capability to follow through on its promises.

EcoStrat-1 — Horizon's first satellite mission — has been confirmed a complete success. The atmospheric and weather monitoring payload achieved its target equatorial orbit and all instruments have checked out nominal. Ground stations are receiving telemetry. The EcoStrat program is now operational.

FOUNDING Year 2026, Day 001
Horizon Aerospace established — SlimeX Space Agency formally dissolved and reconstituted

Following a comprehensive internal review, the board of directors of SlimeX Space Agency has voted to fully dissolve the organisation and reconstitute it under a new name, new leadership structure, and a new operational mandate. The successor agency — Horizon Aerospace — inherits KSC facilities and remaining assets, but carries none of SlimeX's institutional culture. The restructure was described as total: new safety doctrine, new chain of command, new program architecture, and a new identity. The Horizon emblem replaces the SlimeX flag effective immediately. Ultra Horizontem, Ad Astra.

🌅
AGENCY Year 2026, Day 003
Why the rename? Director addresses the Kerbin press

In Horizon Aerospace's first public statement, the Director explained the rationale for the full restructure: "SlimeX had a history. Some of it we're proud of — the Poloria flights, the first orbit, the Mun attempt. But the organisation also had a culture that put milestones ahead of safety. That culture killed Kerbonauts. Horizon does not inherit it."

📢
PROGRAMS Year 2026, Day 018
Two programs announced — EcoStrat satellite initiative and Valkyrie Mun programme

Horizon Aerospace has announced its first two flight programs. EcoStrat will place a series of uncrewed scientific satellites into Kerbin orbit, beginning in Year 2027. The Valkyrie program, structured in three gated phases modelled after historic Earth lunar programmes, will ultimately land Kerbonauts on the Mun — but only after each preceding phase has been reviewed and cleared.

🚀
SAFETY Year 2026, Day 031
Horizon Safety Review Board convenes — independent of flight operations by design

The newly formed Horizon Safety Review Board held its inaugural session at KSC. The board operates entirely independently of the flight operations chain of command and holds veto power over any launch. Its first act was to formalise the gate-based approval structure for Valkyrie: no phase may begin until the previous one is signed off in full. The board will publish its criteria publicly before each mission.

🛡
PERSONNEL Year 2026, Day 044
Kerbonaut selection process opens — applicants must meet new Horizon safety certification standard

Horizon Aerospace has opened applications for the first class of Horizon Kerbonauts. Unlike SlimeX-era recruitment, all candidates must complete the new Horizon Safety Certification programme before being eligible for mission assignment. The programme was developed in consultation with the Kerbonaut Corps and the Orbital Safety Board.

AGENCY VALUES

Three principles guide every decision at Horizon Aerospace — from the drawing board to the launchpad. These are not slogans. They are policies. They are what separates Horizon from what came before.

🛡
01 / 03
SAFETY FIRST

No launch is worth a Kerbal life. Every vehicle passes a full pre-flight review, independent safety audit, and crew sign-off before fuelling begins. We abort when in doubt. A scrubbed launch is a success.

📋
02 / 03
METICULOUS PLANNING

We plan in months, not days. Delta-V budgets, contingency windows, abort trajectories, and crew rotation schedules are finalised before a single part is ordered. Every phase is gated on the last.

🤝
03 / 03
COOPERATION

Space is not a competition. We share data, co-develop hardware, and train alongside partner agencies. The Mun belongs to all Kerbalkind. A mission where everyone comes home matters more than who got there first.
